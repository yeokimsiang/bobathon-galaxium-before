Galaxium Lite: the starting app

This is the lab app as you start with it, running in your browser. Book a flight, then cancel one: the app cancels it, but it can't tell you what you'd get back. In the lab, you and Bob add that.

Starting the app in your browser…

The first visit downloads about 9 MB of Python and takes 10 to 30 seconds. Later visits are quicker.
